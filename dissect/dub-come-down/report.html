Sound design — reverse-engineer

Kick
Sub-heavy balance (sub 9.7, bass 12.3 vs mid 1.27) points to a long-tail house kick doing double duty as sub reinforcement, tuned near F#.

Bass
Bass stem is 85% voiced with 1577 notes centered around F1/F#1/E1 — this is a live-feel, syncopated 16th-note bassline, not a one-note pulse. Range C#1–B3 means occasional octave jumps or fills.

Pad/Harmony
The i–IV (F#m–Bmaj) alternation with occasional D#m and F#maj lifts is the entire harmonic identity. Spectral centroid drops to 1926Hz in the 134s breakdown — pads get filtered hard there.
Prophet 12 detuned saws, slow attack (800ms), lush unison. Play F#m9 → Bmaj9 → D#m11 → F#maj7 as held 4-bar chords. Send to a long plate (4s) + ping-pong delay 3/16 with heavy feedback filtering.
Percussion/Texture
Consistent RMS across sections with high spectral centroid (2900+ in peaks) suggests busy top-end: shakers, closed hats, rides, ticks doing the energy lifting.
Closed hat on off-8ths, open hat on 'and' of 2 and 4, shaker running 16ths with velocity modulation. Add a dub-conga loop bussed to a filtered delay for the classic Freerange/Delusions signature.
Vocal/Lead
Vocal stem is only 32% voiced with 639 notes clustered at F#5 — this is a chopped/looped vocal hook, not a lead vocal. Lyrics transcript is garbage (Whisper hallucinating 'thank you' on reverb tails), confirming it's textural.

Mix moves

  • Aggressive low-mid scoop — the band balance shows mids at 1.27 vs bass 12.3, meaning everything above 300Hz is deliberately dark/restrained. Cut 400-600Hz on pads and vocals to make room for the kick+bass sandwich.
  • Dub delay bus is doing structural work — throws on chord stabs and vocal chops during transitions (esp. the 134s and 232s breakdowns). Use a tape delay with filter automation on the feedback.
  • Sidechain is subtle but present — pump the pads/vocals ~2-3dB off the kick, not the aggressive EDM duck.
  • Master bus is clearly bus-compressed and tape-saturated — the RMS consistency (0.24–0.27 across most of the track) is compressor glue, not natural dynamics.

Arrangement

Nine-section arc: 30s pad intro → 68s groove build (bass+drums) → 34s development → 31s filtered breakdown (centroid drops to 1926Hz, tell-tale HPF sweep) → 67s main peak → 60s dub breakdown (drums thinned, dub FX foregrounded) → 74s final peak → 27s outro tail. The two breakdowns are the transition tricks — both use pad/FX-only passages to reset ear fatigue before re-entry. Very Freerange Records template.

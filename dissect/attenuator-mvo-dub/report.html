Sound design — reverse-engineer

Kick
Steady 4/4 at 129.2bpm, sub-heavy, no snap — sits entirely under 100Hz based on band balance. Kick and bass are effectively fused.
Layer a soft 909-style kick with a sine sub tuned to F1. HPF the click layer at 60Hz, LPF the sub at 90Hz. Glue with light saturation (Saturator, warm mode, drive ~3dB). No transient designer — you want round, not punchy.
Bass
1335 detected notes, range B0–B3, mean around B1/C2 — the pitch tracker is grabbing both the sub fundamental and the chord-stab's low harmonic. Real bassline is likely a simple root movement F–C with passing tones (D#, G#, A appear).
Prophet 12: single sawtooth, one octave, LPF cutoff ~200Hz with slight resonance, slow filter env. Play root notes on downbeats, tie to kick sidechain. Add a second layer, sine an octave down, for the sub floor.
Pad/Harmony
This is the signature MvO chord stab — a filtered detuned pad hitting on the offbeat, drenched in Space Echo-style delay. Fmin↔Cmaj alternation with occasional A#maj coloring is the entire harmonic content.
Prophet 12: 2 detuned sawtooths + 1 square, slow attack (~30ms), medium release. LPF around 800Hz with mild env mod. Bounce to audio, chop into a 1/8 stab, send heavy to a Roland RE-201 emulation (or Echoboy 'Space' mode) with feedback ~55%, wow/flutter on. Add a spring reverb after the delay. Automate delay feedback for the breath sections.
Percussion/Texture
Extremely subdued — high-band energy is 0.35, meaning hats/shakers are barely there or heavily filtered. Any groove comes from the chord-stab syncopation and micro-noise texture.
Closed hat sample, LPF at 4kHz, -18dB in the mix. Add a filtered vinyl noise loop through the same delay bus as the chord. Consider a subtle rimshot on beat 3 with a long plate reverb, ducked by the kick.
Vocal/Lead
Whisper implies the 'Thank you' segments are Whisper hallucinations on delay tails / pad artifacts — not a real vocal. Voiced ratio 40% but the melodic content (F#5, F5 fragments at intro) is likely the pad's high harmonics being read as pitch.
No vocal needed. If you want a lead moment, a single sustained note on Prophet 12 (F5 or C5) through the same delay/reverb chain, ducked -6dB under the pad.

Mix moves

  • Everything above 2kHz gets shelved down 3-6dB — this is a dark mix, resist the urge to add air.
  • Sidechain the pad and bass to the kick with a slow release (~200ms) for that Chain Reaction breathing feel.
  • Send all melodic elements to a single stereo dub bus: tape delay → spring reverb → HPF at 200Hz on the return. Automate the send, not the source.
  • Use a bus compressor (SSL-style, 2:1, slow attack, auto release) across the master with ~2dB GR to lock the plateau feel.

Arrangement

Nine detected sections but functionally it's intro (0-38s) → main plateau A (38-116s) → breath (116-133s, filter opens/pad thins) → plateau B (133-198s, mid brightens) → plateau C peak (198-330s, most energy) → drain 1 (330-364s) → tail (364-419s). Transitions are done by opening/closing the delay feedback and swapping which chord (Fm vs Cmaj) dominates, not by adding elements. The 132-second peak section is the DJ tool zone — this is a mixer's record.

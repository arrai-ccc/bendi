A.02c

✅ Dale vida al sonido (diverged from A.02b)

DONE · TRIGGERS + RACKS + EXPRESIÓN · THU 2026-06-11
Qué pasó de verdad

Bendi trajo un beat (bueno) + una referencia house con pads rítmicos preciosos. Felipe agarró ese pad como norte y montó la clase sobre la herida de Bendi: sus pads suenan planos y no sabe por qué. El sidechain volvió como gate-trigger (puente C1→C2). El descubrimiento del día: un pad plano no está roto — le falta movimiento y expresión. Full record → class-log.md Class 2.

Qué cubrimos
  • Instrumentos como señal de trigger — un kit de 808 como señal (no como sonido) para disparar efectos en otra pista.
  • Gate disparado por el 808 sobre un pad con acordes → pad plano se vuelve rítmico, vivo. Esculpir su forma eligiendo qué dispara.
  • Audio Effect Racks — reverb+sat+filtro / delays / Roar (Bendi armó el suyo). Macros = una mano sobre el carácter.
  • MIDI Effect Racks — Arp · Scale · Chord · Note Echo en cadena, tocando piano encima → ideas que no escribirías a mano.
  • El sonido como personaje — darle herramientas para expresarse (etapas, momentos, agresivo/tierno/crujiente).
  • Duplicar trigger + jugar con los espacios — apagar las notas originales y rellenar los huecos → capas que se interlockean y rebotan.
Salsa entregada
  • Cualquier instrumento puede ser la llave (no solo el kick) — cada trigger esculpe un ritmo distinto sobre el pad.
  • Rellena los huecos, no los repitas — el groove vive en lo que no suena.
  • Un pad plano no está roto, le falta moverse — gate/filtro/envelope antes de cambiar preset.
  • Un macro = una mano sobre el sonido; guarda el rack en User Library.
  • Apila MIDI FX y deja que la cadena proponga; baja a audio el oro.
  • Roar multibanda = peso en los medios-graves sin ensuciar el sub.
Deberes

One Beat Per Day + reto movimiento (haz mover un pad) + ejercicio de espacios (dos capas que se rellenen). Traer otro beat a C3.

Diferido

Kick/bass frequency-fitting (el core original de A.02b) NO fue el foco — reabsorber en sesión futura o en la mitad de mezcla. Abierto: confirmar Live edition de Bendi en su casa (usó Roar en el estudio = Suite de Felipe; su setup propio sigue sin confirmar).

A.03

▶ Deep dive: un solo device a fondo

DEEP DIVE · 1.5–2H · THU 2026-06-18 · RE-SEQUENCED
El cambio (Felipe's call 06-11)

C2 tocó muchas herramientas en la superficie (gates, racks, MIDI FX, Roar). C3 hace lo contrario: un solo device, hasta el fondo. Bendi aprende el método de dominar una herramienta a fondo — transferible a todas las demás. Device = curiosidad de Bendi (pregunta de deberes) + elección de Felipe.

Recomendación ARC — Operator (FM, Suite)
  • Secuela natural de C2: C2 movió el pad desde fuera (gate/racks/MIDI FX) → Operator enseña el movimiento desde dentro (envelopes por operador, LFO, filtro, FM). Cierra la herida del pad en la raíz.
  • Un device profundo > tres synths superficiales. FM es la esquina que todos evitan — desmitificarla es alto-leverage. Stock Suite, coste €0.
  • Qué hacemos: 4 osciladores + algoritmos (carrier/modulador), envelopes por operador (ahí nace el movimiento), filtro + su envelope, LFO. Construir un pad evolutivo y un bajo/lead con carácter desde un patch init.
  • El método (transferible): init → un oscilador → moldea su envelope → añade modulador → movimiento de filtro → guardar. El mismo ritual para cualquier device.
Alt — Echo (delay)

Si se inclina a efectos: Echo como instrumento creativo, no utilidad — feedback hasta auto-oscilar, filtro en el loop, ducking, modulación, blend de reverb. Convierte un loop seco en espacio dub-techno. Liga con el tema "movimiento y expresión" de C2.

Salsa
  • Arranca siempre de init/default, nunca de un preset — solo aprendes construyendo desde cero.
  • Un parámetro bien automatizado > diez estáticos (lección de movimiento de C2 dentro del synth).
  • Resamplea a audio cuando esté — lo compromete, libera CPU, corta el tweaking infinito.
  • Guarda patch + FX rack juntos en User Library — así un "signature sound" se vuelve reutilizable.
Deberes

One Beat Per Day; un sonido al día construido desde cero en el device de la clase. Traer otro beat + decir qué device/efecto le pica la curiosidad (alimenta la elección).

A.04

Armonía aplicada

ARMONÍA · 1.5–2H · THU 2026-06-25
Objetivo

He already knows grados, escalas, triadas (studied with El Chiru). Translate theory → his music: a progression that moves and sustains tension without cliché, on one of his loops.

Qué hacemos
  • Build a progression that holds tension — why a 4-note chord sounds more mature than a triad; voice-leading; tension/release at the harmonic level.
  • Bass-line vs chord relationship; inversions for smooth movement.
  • Translate his theory knowledge into hands-on Ableton moves.
Ableton 12 (this is where 12 saves hours — teach + refresh-myself)
  • Scale awareness / Key & Scales — set the clip scale, off-scale rows dim, stay diatonic effortlessly.
  • MIDI Generator → Stacks — generate chords/progressions from the scale (root, inversion, duration). Harmonic scaffolding in seconds.
  • MIDI Transformation → Strum (MPE) — chord rolls (the Floating Points shimmer); Recombine for instant melodic variations from one good bar; Span to make a progression legato (pad) or staccato (pluck).
  • Expressive Chords (12.2) — build progressions one key at a time, then feed into Stacks/Recombine.
Salsa
  • Write the progression once, then Recombine it 4 times → 4 variations for different sections, all in key.
  • An unlinked clip envelope on a chord's filter (Class 1 callback) makes a static pad evolve across the whole 8-bar phrase.
  • Add the 7th/9th last — write the triad skeleton first, color after.
Deberes

One Beat Per Day with a harmonic focus — one progression per day using scale-mode + Stacks.

B.05

De loop a arrangement

ARRANGEMENT · 1.5–2H · THU 2026-07-02
Objetivo

"El paso más difícil" — and Bendi's core wound (the guardador profesional who never finishes). The most important session of the front half. Take one of his most promising loops to a 5–6 minute arrangement.

Qué hacemos
  • Structure as tensión y resolución — when to build, when to drop, where the breaks go, when to let it breathe.
  • The decision framework: what comes after the first 16 bars; when to add, when to subtract.
  • Drag the Session loops (built across Classes 1–4) into the Arrangement; shape the energy curve.
Ableton 12
  • Arrangement editing shortcuts — Duplicate Time (⌘⇧D) to repeat a section, Insert Silence (⌘I) to open a gap, Consolidate (⌘J).
  • Take lanes (Class 1 callback) for recorded filter-performance automation across the build.
  • Locators to mark intro / build / drop / break / outro.
Salsa
  • Arrange by subtraction: lay the full loop across 5 min first, then remove elements to create the intro/breakdown. Easier than building from nothing.
  • The arrangement is a series of irreversible decisions — this is the antidote to his hoarding pattern. Commit sections to audio so he can't keep reopening them.
  • One transition trick per section (a reverse cymbal, a filter sweep, a 1-bar silence before the drop) is enough — don't over-produce transitions.
Deberes

Finish (rough is fine) the arrangement of the Class-5 track before Class 6. The point is done, not perfect.

After Class 5

Classes 6–7 = the two mix sessions (clean/space/frequencies, then character/glue/dynamics — gain staging, EQ quirúrgico, FabFilter Q3/Pro-MB, compresión, saturación, SoundToys/Valhalla). Class 8 = pre-master, export, ciclo review, decide second-ciclo-or-pause.

Lo que cubrimos
  1. Drum Rack — how it works, loading samples into pads, building a first rhythm.
  2. Built the first rhythm on the Drum Rack using Splice samples.
  3. Choke mode (choke groups) — pads that cut each other off.
  4. Take lanes in the Arrangement while looping a section — capturing multiple passes.
  5. The "versatile" track in Session View — one go-to track to record loops and manage ideas fast.
  6. Clip Envelopes — Linked vs Unlinked mode.
  7. The Splice engine — searching, auditioning, drag-drop inside Live.
  8. Grooves / Groove Pool — feel and swing.
Extra — adjacent, show when natural
  • Capture MIDI as the safety net: noodle with nothing armed, hit Capture, Live grabs the last phrase, detects tempo, sets the loop. Pairs perfectly with the "versatile track" idea — he never loses a good bar.
  • Drum Rack return chains (up to 6) — parallel processing inside the rack (a parallel-saturation bus, a reverb-throw bus per pad).
  • Macros on the Drum Rack (up to 16) — map cutoff + drive + reverb-send to one knob for a live one-knob build.
  • Commit a groove — once the feel is right, Commit writes it into the clip (MIDI notes move; audio gets warp markers).
Salsa — the non-obvious pro tips to hand Bendi
  • Choke beyond hats: put a long evolving sound (noise sweep, vinyl crackle, a sustained texture) in a choke group with itself across two pads — retriggering chops it rhythmically. Free stutter, no effect. Also choke a texture pad against a "silence" sample to gate atmospheres on demand.
  • Don't choke kick + snare together — choke is a hard cut, wrong for two simultaneous core hits. Choke is for one-at-a-time families (hats, tails, sweeps).
  • Take-lane comping: use Audition Mode (headphone icon on a take lane) to hear a take in context before committing. Turn on Create Fades on Clip Edges so comped slices auto-crossfade (4 ms) — no clicks. Comping works for MIDI too (loop-record an improvised line, comp the best phrases).
  • Unlinked clip envelopes = Ableton's built-in polyrhythmic LFO. A 1-bar loop with an 8-bar unlinked filter-cutoff envelope evolves over 8 bars while the notes repeat every bar — never static. Use odd/prime lengths (3, 5, 7 bars against a 1–2 bar clip) for Mullaert-style evolving phasing. Stack several at different lengths (cutoff 7 / reverb-send 5 / pan 3) for generative movement with zero automation lanes.
  • Groove sauce: extract a groove off a swung hat loop and apply it only to the bassline at ~50–70% Timing → bass locks into the hat's pocket ("everything breathes together"). Keep Random low (5–15%) — humanizes without smearing the kick. Watch the Base value: wrong base = swing on the wrong subdivision.
  • Splice "Search with Sound": drag a clip from his own session into the Splice panel → Splice returns loops that match his track's key + groove. Reverse-search instead of keyword-hunting. Auditions are tempo-synced and key-conformed before he commits.
Deberes
  • One Beat Per Day (Mathew Jonson / Freedom Engine): 15–45 min daily, one loop/idea, save it, move on. Quality irrelevant — it's the creative-state muscle. Folder /one-beat-per-day/YYYY-MM-DD/. Rule isn't 365/365 — it's never more than 2 days without sitting down.
  • Bring one of those beats (or a backlog idea) to Class 2 with the rhythm started.

⚠ Accuracy flags (caught in research)

"Bite" and "Gentle Compressor" are NOT native Live devices. "Bite" = 3rd-party M4L bitcrusher; native bitcrush is Redux; native dynamics are Compressor / Glue Compressor / Multiband Dynamics ("Gentle Squeeze" is an old preset name). Don't teach these as native.

04 — Shortcuts

Ableton Live 12 — Cheatsheet (macOS)

Verified against the official Live 12 Reference Manual. Windows differs only where noted (beyond ⌘→Ctrl / ⌥→Alt). Hover (or tap) any chip for the explanation.

⚠ #1 beginner gotcha

Single-letter shortcuts (B, K, S, C, O, Q, R, W, H…) only fire when the Computer MIDI Keyboard is OFF — toggle with M. If it's on, hold ⇧ + the letter. Some toggles also latch momentarily — hold ~500 ms, release to snap back.
